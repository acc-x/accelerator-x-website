Questions

The questions executives ask.

  • Why from {{offering:senior-leader-acceleration.price_from_gbp}} for 1:1 when a cohort place is {{offering:leadership-cohort.price_from_gbp}}?

    Fair question. A cohort place buys you a seat in a room of 10–12: shared time, a set pace, a shared curriculum. The 1:1 is the opposite: a founder's undivided attention for six weeks, an engagement built entirely around your role and your stack, moving at your pace, with us doing the work alongside you rather than teaching to a group. It's also opportunity cost, that time would otherwise fill a whole cohort. Premium, scarce, and bespoke. If the cohort economics suit you better, we'll say so.

  • How is this different from a business coach or AI training?

    A coach asks good questions; a trainer teaches a syllabus. We do neither in isolation; we build, with you. By the end you have proprietary workflows, custom instructions and personal AI assistants running in your real work, tuned to how you lead and the company's tech stack. The promise is we take things off your plate, not add something new to learn and do.

  • Will my work stay confidential?

    Completely. 1:1 work is confidential: nothing is shared across clients, no case studies, no names. What you build is yours to keep and run. We handle NDAs directly with you where needed.

  • Can I choose my founder-coach?

    Tell us what you're working on and we'll match you to the right founder: Toby leads on strategy and the partnership, Andy on product, architecture and hands-on implementation. Either way you get a dedicated founder for the full six weeks, not a rotating bench.

  • What if it's not working, can I stop?

    Yes: no value, no payment. You get two full sessions and the resources are yours to keep; if it's not for you, cancel before the third and you owe nothing. Beyond that there's no lock-in. We'd rather earn your confidence than hold you to a contract.

  • Is there a call before I commit, and what about rescheduling?

    Always a chemistry call first to confirm fit: we only take on engagements we're confident in. Once you start, the sessions flex around your calendar and the continuous async support carries on between them, so a moved session never stalls the work.
