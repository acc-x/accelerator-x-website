Questions

Asked at week 2, week 8, and week 16.

  • Can we skip Phase 0?

    No. Phase 0 is the diagnostic that makes everything else accurate. Without it, Phase 1 is built on assumptions, ours or yours. The two weeks are the fastest way to stop wasting time on the wrong things. Every engagement starts here.

  • How firm is the {{offering:company-enablement.phases.0.price_from_gbp}} price?

    It's the floor for a standard engagement: leadership team of up to 10, one site, no significant travel. For larger organisations, multi-site or international scope, the price reflects the complexity. We'll scope it precisely before you commit to anything.

  • What's the '+' in Phase 1+?

    The number of cycles is determined by your roadmap, not a pre-packaged scope. Some organisations need two cycles. Some need six. The '+' means we run as many as your roadmap calls for, and stop when you're self-sufficient, not when a contract runs out.

  • Can we run cycles in parallel?

    Not typically. Sequential cycles are the model by design: each one builds internal capability that the next one depends on. Running two simultaneously means splitting founder attention and slowing down the people-development that makes the work stick. We'll tell you honestly if there's a case for parallel tracks.

  • Who owns the IP?

    You do. Everything built during the engagement (the systems, the prompts, the workflows, the trained operators) belongs to you from day one. That's not a nice-to-have. It's the point. We leave nothing that requires us to keep the lights on.

  • What happens after the last cycle?

    Most clients taper to a light monthly advisory: senior judgement on tap, without a delivery team embedded. Some close out entirely: their internal operators are running it and they don't need us. Either outcome is a success, and we'll tell you which one we think you're heading toward before the final cycle begins.
