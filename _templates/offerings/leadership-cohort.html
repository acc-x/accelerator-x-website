What you build

Your Chief of Staff is the first system.

It starts with a folder and a few files, something you already know how to use. Each week adds sophistication, like connected tools and scheduled tasks. You leave with a working system and the toolkit to build the next one, anywhere in your work or life.

The people who get ahead are not the ones chasing the latest tool or trend. They understand the principles of how these tools work, so they can build a system that is personal to them, their tools and their business.

Questions

The questions cohort applicants ask.

  • How much time does this take?

    90 minutes a week live, the three-hour in-person session in week 5, and some practice in between. The practice is building your own system, so it pays back as you go. If your diary genuinely won't flex for a weekly session, apply for the next cohort when the timing is better.

  • Will my company expense this?

    Most cohort members expense this or put it through professional development budgets. At {{offering:leadership-cohort.price_from_gbp}}, it sits under many L&D thresholds without board approval. We can provide an invoice addressed to your company and a one-page brief on what the programme covers.

  • What if a competitor of mine applies?

    We vet every application with this in mind. If we identify a conflict at application stage, we'll flag it with both parties before confirming either place. Once a cohort is confirmed, every member has signed off on the non-competing composition.

  • Can my whole team join?

    No: this is a director/function-owner programme designed for the person who owns the AI agenda, not their team. If you want to bring AI capability across a whole team or organisation, our Company Enablement track is designed for that.

  • What if I miss a session?

    We ask you not to. Eight weekly sessions: that's the commitment. If something unavoidable comes up, we'll share the session recording. But the peer value is live, and you can't replay a room.

  • Is there a guarantee?

    Yes: no value, no payment. You get two full sessions and the resources are yours to keep; if it's not for you, tell us before the third and you owe nothing. There's also a pre-cohort interview (a 1:1 with a founder) before we confirm your place, so we only proceed if it's a genuine fit on both sides.

  • Do I need to be technical?

    No. There's no code in this room. You build with Claude, in plain language, around your own work. We teach the system architecture underneath so you understand what you've built and can extend it yourself.

  • Do I have to come in person?

    It's the best way to do week 5, but it's not required. Participants have travelled from across the UK and abroad, and many are London-based. The venue and date are still to be confirmed. If you can't be there, you join live by video, and everyone gets the recording and all the session materials.

  • Do I need the latest agent tools I keep hearing about?

    No. Many of those tools are a distraction if you don't understand the architecture underneath. Once you know how to use Claude properly, you can build a more capable personal agent, and a whole agentic system, that you control and can extend to any part of your work or life.

  • How big is the cohort?

    Small by design. We expect between 10 and 15 senior leaders, and we vet every application so the room stays non-competing.

  • Is it only for Claude?

    We build in Claude because it's what we run our own business on. The building blocks carry across to any AI tool.

  • What if my company locks down email and diary?

    Tell us before you join. We can set you up on your own files, and the building blocks are the same.

  • Do I get a certificate?

    No. You get a working system on your own work, which is more useful.
