Questions

The questions leadership teams ask.

  • What does this cost for, say, 8 leaders?

    It's a base plus per-head: {{offering:leadership-activation.price_from_gbp}} for up to 8 people, then £2,000 per person above that, to a maximum of 12, so a team of 8 is £14,000 (about £1,750 per person), all +VAT. Tell us your group size and we'll confirm the exact figure.

  • How is this different from Company Enablement, when both work with our leaders?

    The most common confusion, so thank you for naming it. Company Enablement moves your whole business: it starts by aligning leadership but its job is to activate the wider team and ship capability across the org, cycle by cycle. This is narrower and deeper: it builds the AI capability of your leadership group itself. Rule of thumb: Company Enablement for org-wide change; this for upskilling your leaders specifically.

  • What's the difference between this and a one-off workshop?

    A workshop is one good day; capability doesn't stick from one afternoon. This is eight weeks of bi-weekly half-days where your team applies DOTS to your real context and builds a shared way of working: you leave with a team-level artefact and aligned leadership, not just notes. If you do want a single high-impact session, that's our Talks & Events leadership offsite.

  • How do you tailor it to our business?

    Entirely. We come to you, on-site, and the work runs on your real priorities and confidential context, not a generic curriculum. DOTS is the engine; what goes through it is your business.

  • What if it's not working, is there a guarantee?

    Yes: no value, no payment. You get two full sessions and the resources are yours to keep; if it's not for you, stop before the third and you owe nothing. Beyond that there's no lock-in.

  • What do we own at the end?

    One shared AI operating model, a team-level artefact your leaders built together, and aligned leadership that keeps moving without us. What you build is yours.
