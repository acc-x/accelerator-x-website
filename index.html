Honest about fit

Is this for you?

Right fit if

  • You know AI needs to happen, but you don't yet have a clear path
  • You want something shipped, not a strategy deck
  • You're open to honest conversations about what's getting in the way
  • You're a decision-maker who can put the right people in the room

Probably not for you if

  • The person who can make this happen isn't available to be actively involved
  • You want AI to slot into the business without asking the team to change how they work
