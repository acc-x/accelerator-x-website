# Bootcamp blog shortlist

Drafted 21 November 2026 for founder review. Nothing here is published, scheduled or sent.

**Source material read:** Cohort 3 participant companions for sessions 1 to 3, the Session 4 first draft, Cohort 3 delivery and catch-up notes (6, 10, 13 and 18 November), the living programme-direction note (20 November), the proof-and-testimonials file, and the Cohort 4 briefing. Cohorts 1 and 2 were not needed.

**Target reader:** a founder, CEO or MD of a UK business of tens to a few hundred people who has tried AI and stalled. Not a builder.

**Rules applied:** no participant names, no survey numbers (none are public except the cohort page's "9.2 out of 10", which none of the drafts uses), and no course text reproduced. Each piece gives one idea and one illustrative worked example, and ends with one next step.

**Independent review, 21 November.** A fresh-context reviewer checked every claim against the sources. Its blocking findings were fixed before the PR:
- 022's anecdote had picked up detail the notes don't support (a duration, the room's reaction, "nothing was lost"). It is now cut back to what the 18 November catch-up records, with no gender.
- Toby's first-person career line is now general.
- 021 had made "in our experience" claims and reproduced Session 3's selection table, candidate bank and scoring criteria. It is rewritten around the one supported room observation (the same prompt gave very different outputs) and one example method.
- 020 overstated that Claude always reads `CLAUDE.md`, and its rules-file example lifted lines from the companion. Both are reworded.

Left for founders: whether 020's interview prompt gives away too much of the Session 1 interview prompt, and Toby's own voice pass on 022.

## The ten angles

| # | Working title | The one-sentence point | Draws on | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Give AI a desk, not a question** *(drafted: 020)* | Output needs rework because every chat starts from zero; say who you are once, give each recurring job a folder with a short rules file, and the prompt only carries what is new. | Session 1 (instructions, folder, CLAUDE.md, "context stacks") | Andy |

| 3 | **Check the file, not the reply** *(drafted: 022)* | An assistant saying "done" is a claim, not evidence; three quick checks and a written boundary make it safe to delegate to. | Session 2 and its 18 November catch-up (the save that wasn't), Session 2 permission rules | Toby |

| 10 | From single-player to multiplayer: when your method becomes the team's | The bigger unlock is not one leader working faster but a method one person built being used by colleagues, so a job is done the same way across the business. | Programme direction note (Andy, 20 Nov) | Toby |

## Why these three first

They are the three ideas the room has already tested (sessions 1 to 3 have run), each gives the reader something to do in under an hour, and together they follow the order a leader would build in: context, then saved methods, then trust. Angles 7 and 10 draw on sessions that have not run yet; write them after Session 4 and Session 5 so they rest on what happened, not on the plan.
